Live Train Tracker Guide

🚆 Real-Time Tracking & Kinematics

This interactive map visualizes the MBTA Franklin/Foxboro Commuter Rail Line in real time across all 24 stations. Vehicle positions, speeds, and upcoming station ETAs refresh every 10 seconds directly from the official MBTA V3 API. A 60 FPS kinematic engine smoothly glides active trains along the track between updates.

🔄 Dynamic Fairmount Route

During off-peak, weekend, and late-night hours, certain trains are scheduled via the Fairmount Line branch (stopping at Newmarket, Uphams Corner, Four Corners/Geneva, Talbot Ave, Morton St, Blue Hill Ave, and Fairmount) rather than the Southwest Corridor (Back Bay, Ruggles, Forest Hills, Hyde Park). The map displays all 24 stations and dynamically highlights the Fairmount Route whenever trains are actively running on it!

🔀 Route Junctions & Termini

Trains depart Boston's South Station along either the Southwest Corridor or the Fairmount Line, converging at Readville (which also serves as a terminus for Fairmount Line and late-night short-turn service) before continuing through Endicott, Dedham Corporate Center, Islington, and Norwood to Walpole. At Walpole, the line forks into the upper branch to Forge Park/495 (via Norfolk & Franklin) and the lower branch to Foxboro.

🎨 Direction & Color Coding

  • Cyan / INBOUND: Trains traveling inbound toward Boston South Station.
  • Amber / OUTBOUND: Trains traveling outbound toward Forge Park/495, Foxboro, or Readville.

🖱️ Interactive Controls

• Click any station circle: Open a popover with upcoming scheduled and predicted departures in both directions.
• Click or hover any train marker or bottom card: Synchronizes selection, reveals the floating tooltip, and displays real-time speed, boarding countdowns, and upcoming stops.
